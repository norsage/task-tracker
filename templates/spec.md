## Context

What's the situation? What's known, what's already been tried, what prompted
this work? Link prior tasks, specs, papers, or notes.

## Question / Goal

What do we want to learn or build? Phrase as a question for research work,
as an outcome for engineering work. Keep it to one paragraph.

## Approach

How do we plan to attack it? Methods, candidate experiments, architecture
sketch. List alternatives considered and why this one.

## Out of scope

Things we explicitly are *not* doing in this spec. Helps future-you avoid
scope creep and lets reviewers calibrate.

## Risks & unknowns

What could make this not work, or change direction? Data we don't have,
assumptions that might break, things we'd have to revisit if X happens.

## Evaluation

How will we know we're learning something / making progress? For research:
measurements, baselines, ablations we'll run. For engineering: behaviour
we expect to verify. Avoid binary success criteria; describe signals.

## References

Papers, prior work, related specs / tasks, external tickets.
